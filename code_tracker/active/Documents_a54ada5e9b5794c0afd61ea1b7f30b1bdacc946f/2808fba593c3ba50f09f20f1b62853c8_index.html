Built for Privacy by Default

Kryptos is designed from the ground up to ensure absolute security, zero trust, and total anonymity.

End-to-End Encryption

Mathematical certainty. All packets are encrypted on the client side before they ever touch the network.

Anonymous Routing

Your IP address is masked through multi-hop onion routing, ensuring untraceable communication.

Local Key Ownership

Cryptographic keypairs never leave your device. You are your own root of trust.

Zero Central Authority

No master servers, DNS kill switches, or corporate ownership. A purely democratic protocol.

Zero Trust Architecture

Trust no one, verify everything. Every node is treated as potentially compromised by default.
